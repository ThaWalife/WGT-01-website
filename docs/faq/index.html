Does it work with my version of Photoshop?

WGT-01 is tested on Photoshop 27.9.1. Earlier versions will be tested later.

Is the panel in English?

Not yet: the panel is in Italian. These docs give the English meaning of every control next to its label.

Does it work with CMYK or 16-bit documents?

Not for now: you need an 8-bit RGB document. Halftone, however, also exports to SVG in CMYK.

The panel is slow on very large images

Contour lines and Flow lines do a lot of calculation. Try your settings on a selection, then apply to the whole image.

“Select Subject” freezes Photoshop

On some images Photoshop’s automatic selection hangs. That’s why in Type the default is La mia selezione (my selection): select the subject yourself before laying out.

Can I use the results for commercial work?

Yes. The images you create with WGT-01 are yours.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The plugin works in Photoshop on your computer, and the site’s try-it page also computes everything in your browser.
