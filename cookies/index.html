Cookies and privacy

What this site stores, what it doesn’t, and how to change your choice.

In short

  • The WGT-01 website doesn’t use profiling or analytics cookies, and sets no cookies of its own.
  • There are no ads and no tracking pixels. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn’t recognise you from one visit to the next: it only gives overall numbers (page views, countries, browsers).
  • The “Notify me at launch” newsletter is optional: your email goes to MailerLite only if you type it and press Subscribe.
  • Typefaces are hosted on the site itself: opening the pages sends no requests to Google or other font services.
  • Photos you try on the Try it page are processed only in your browser: they aren’t sent or saved anywhere.
  • The only optional services are third-party ones (today: the Payhip payment button, once downloads open) and they start only if you accept them.

Who is responsible

The site is a personal project by Walife (thawalife.com). For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, write to thawalife@gmail.com.

Visit statistics

To know how many people visit the site and from which countries, pages load a small Cloudflare Web Analytics script (Cloudflare, Inc.). It stores nothing on your device: no cookies, no local storage, no browser “fingerprint”. Cloudflare receives the IP address and technical details of the visit (page, browser, operating system, country derived from the IP) and gives them back to the site owner only as totals: nobody can trace them back to you or follow you across sites. That’s why no consent is needed. If you’d still rather avoid it, any content blocker (for example uBlock Origin) stops it without breaking the site. Policy: Cloudflare Web Analytics and privacy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

“Notify me at launch” newsletter

If you leave your email in the form, I receive it through MailerLite (MailerLite Limited, Ireland, data stored in the European Union). It’s used only to write to you when WGT-01 is available and when a new version comes out.

  • Double opt-in: you get an email with a link; you’re not subscribed until you confirm.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR, Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time with the “unsubscribe” link at the bottom of every email or by writing to thawalife@gmail.com.
  • I keep your email for as long as you stay subscribed; MailerLite also records the date, time and IP of sign-up and confirmation, as proof of consent.
  • The form loads no MailerLite scripts: data is sent only when you press Subscribe.

Policy: MailerLite Privacy Policy.

Hosting

The site is published with GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub receives the IP address and technical details of the request (browser, requested page, time) to show you the pages and protect the service. The site owner has no access to this data. Policy: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Links to other sites (for example thawalife.com, Payhip, MailerLite or Adobe’s documentation) take you off this site: there, their own policies apply.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Articles 15–22) you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its use or object to its processing. If you think your data is being handled improperly you can contact the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or the authority in your country.

This page follows the Italian Garante’s guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools (10 June 2021) and the EU ePrivacy directive.
