Type lays out text over your image the way a designer would: it chooses format, colours, typefaces and composition, and creates **real, editable text layers**.

## 1. Text

Write each part in its own box: kicker, title, subtitle, body, details and call to action. **Only the title is required.**

- **Punteggiatura tipografica** (typographic punctuation): curly apostrophes and quotes, proper dashes and ellipses.
- **Niente parole isolate** (no orphans): in long text the last two words stay together.

## 2. Context

The kind of communication (concert, culture, promo, corporate, sport, food, party) and the tone (elegant, energetic, institutional, playful, underground) are recognised from the words in your text. You can change them yourself.

## 3. Format

Type recognises the format from the document: 4:5 post, square, 9:16 story, poster, banner. In **stories** it keeps clear the top and bottom areas covered by Instagram’s interface. In **posters** the minimum size is 9 pt. With **Corpo del testo** (text size) you scale all the text up or down.

## 4. Colours

- **La mia palette** (my palette): up to 5 brand colours (#RRGGBB). The first becomes the background.
- **Dall’immagine** (from the image): colours are taken from the photo, with faithful, complementary, analogous or monochrome harmonies.

Contrast is always readable: at least 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for titles. Over photos it’s measured at the worst point under the text and, if needed, Type adds a veil or a backing panel.

## 5. Typefaces

Type analyses the fonts installed on your computer and classifies them on its own, then suggests pairs that suit the tone. Symbol fonts and non-Latin alphabets are excluded. The first **Analizza i miei font** (analyse my fonts) can take a while if you have many.

## 6. Layout

Six archetypes, on a 6- or 12-column grid:

| Archetype | When it works |
|---|---|
| **Asse centrale** (centre axis) | Subject in the middle, text below or above. |
| **Bandiera a sinistra** (flush left) | Left-aligned text, classic reading. |
| **Titolo gigante** (giant title) | The title is the star. |
| **Colonna laterale** (side column) | Lots of text, photo to keep clear. |
| **Testo nella zona calma** (text in the quiet zone) | The text goes where the photo is most even. |
| **Striscia** (band) | A solid band carries the text. |

**Soggetto da evitare** (subject to avoid): the default is **La mia selezione** (my selection): you select the subject before laying out. The automatic option with *Select Subject* has to be switched on by hand.

## The result

Type creates a “{{name}} Type” group above the selected layer, with editable text layers and, if you like, the grid guides (**Aggiungi le guide**, add guides). It’s a single step in the history: Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo.
