## Before you start

Select the **image layer** you want to work on. If you also make a selection, {{name}} works only on that area.

## Presets

Every section has its own **Presets**: ready-made combinations of settings to start from and then fine-tune.

## Live preview

With **Anteprima live** (live preview) on, you see the result while you move the controls. The preview is computed in a temporary tab (.psb), always at ×1, so your document doesn’t change until you confirm.

## The result

Every result becomes a **Smart Object** above the starting layer. With **Mantieni livello originale** (keep original layer) the starting image stays in the document; with **Sfondo trasparente** (transparent background) the result has an empty background.

## Edit and All layers

- **Modifica** (edit) reopens a selected {{name}} result with its settings, so you can change them.
- **Tutti i livelli** (all layers) applies the current settings to every visible image layer, one by one.

Details are in [Edit and working on several layers](/doc/modifica-e-livelli/).

## Theme

The panel follows Photoshop’s theme: dark or light.
