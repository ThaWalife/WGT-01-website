## What {{name}} is

{{name}} is a plugin for Adobe Photoshop. It reads the **greyscale** of a layer (or a selection) and uses it to generate graphics: every dark, mid or light area becomes something different.

The panel has three sections:

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Pattern](/doc/pattern/) | Repeats an element (a shape or your own file) and sets its size and position from the tone. |
| [Styles](/doc/stili/index/) | Ten generative styles: Dither, Halftone, ASCII, Mosaic, Engraving, Flow lines, Contour lines, Gradient map, Glitch, Pixel sorting. |
| [Type](/doc/type/) | Lays out the text of posters, posts, stories and banners: format, colours, typefaces and composition. |

## Where to start

1. [Install the plugin](/doc/installazione/).
2. Read [The panel](/doc/il-pannello/): presets, preview, Edit and All layers work the same way in every section.
3. Open a photo, select its layer and try a section.

> **The panel is in Italian for now.** In these pages each control is written with its label as it appears in the panel, in bold, followed by its meaning in English.
