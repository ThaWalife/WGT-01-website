## 0.16.5

- **Soggetto da evitare** (subject to avoid): the default is “La mia selezione” (my selection); the automatic option with *Select Subject* is switched on by hand, with a check that prevents freezes.

## 0.16.4

- Text is written in boxes, one per role.
- The user’s selection always counts; covering the subject weighs more in choosing the composition.

## 0.16.1 – 0.16.3

- Fixes after the first tests: one layout at a time, correct font measurements, more reliable LM Studio.

## 0.16

- New **Type** section: automatic layout of posters, posts, stories and banners.
- **Dev option** with LM Studio.

## 0.15

- New algorithms for **Contour lines** and **Flow lines**, with new controls and presets.

## 0.14

- **SVG export**.
- New styles: Contour lines, Flow lines, Pixel sorting, Gradient map.

## 0.12

- **Edit** and **All layers**.
- Pattern: variations, different elements per tone, distributions, multicolour.
