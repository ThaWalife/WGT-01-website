## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dimensione tessera** (tile size) | Size of the tiles. |
| **Fuga** (grout) | Space between one tile and the next. |
| **Rilievo** (relief) | Light and shadow on the tile edges. |

## Common adjustments

Every style starts from the **Regolazioni** (adjustments): **Luminosità** (brightness) and **Contrasto** (contrast) change the greyscale before the style reads it, **Inverti toni** (invert tones) swaps lights and darks.

## Tip

Large tiles with thin grout give a tiled-wall look; small tiles give soft pixel art.
