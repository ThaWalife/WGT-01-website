## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Distanza tra le linee** (line spacing) | Minimum space between two lines. |
| **Densità segue il tono** (density follows tone) | How much the lines crowd together in the shadows. |
| **Spessore massimo** (maximum width) | Line thickness in the darkest areas. |
| **Lunghezza massima** (maximum length) | Beyond this length a line stops. |
| **Morbidezza del flusso** (flow smoothness) | How much the directions are smoothed: high = wide curves. |
| **Variante** (variant) | Changes the lines’ starting points. |

## Common adjustments

Every style starts from the **Regolazioni** (adjustments): **Luminosità** (brightness) and **Contrasto** (contrast) change the greyscale before the style reads it, **Inverti toni** (invert tones) swaps lights and darks.

## Tip

On large photos the calculation takes a few seconds: work on a selection first to find your settings.
