## Visit statistics {#statistiche}

To know how many people visit the site and from which countries, pages load a small **Cloudflare Web Analytics** script (Cloudflare, Inc.). It stores nothing on your device: no cookies, no local storage, no browser “fingerprint”. Cloudflare receives the IP address and technical details of the visit (page, browser, operating system, country derived from the IP) and gives them back to the site owner **only as totals**: nobody can trace them back to you or follow you across sites. That’s why no consent is needed. If you’d still rather avoid it, any content blocker (for example uBlock Origin) stops it without breaking the site. Policy: [Cloudflare Web Analytics and privacy](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) and [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

If you leave your email in the form, I receive it through **MailerLite** (MailerLite Limited, Ireland, data stored in the European Union). It’s used **only** to write to you when {{name}} is available and when a new version comes out.

- **Double opt-in**: you get an email with a link; you’re not subscribed until you confirm.
- Legal basis: your consent (GDPR, Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time with the “unsubscribe” link at the bottom of every email or by writing to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}).
- I keep your email for as long as you stay subscribed; MailerLite also records the date, time and IP of sign-up and confirmation, as proof of consent.
- The form loads no MailerLite scripts: data is sent only when you press Subscribe.

Policy: [MailerLite Privacy Policy](https://www.mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy).
